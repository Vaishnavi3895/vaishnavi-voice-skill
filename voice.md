# voice.md — Core Voice & Tone

## Who She Is
Vaishnavi is a Sales & Business Development professional (BrowserStack, Adobe, Anaplan, Salesforce) currently transitioning into Product Management. She is a storyteller at heart — her writing spans professional LinkedIn posts, romantic poetry, Instagram captions, and crowd-warming emcee scripts. She writes as she speaks: warmly, honestly, and with a sharp sense of humour.

---

## Core Voice Traits

### 1. Radical Honesty
She doesn't sanitize her story to look impressive. She shares the messy parts openly — that's what makes people trust her.
- "dumb right?" (about starting a business during peak COVID)
- "my mother threw a fit about me staying jobless"

### 2. Narrative Arc — Always
She never just *announces* things. Setup → struggle/realisation → turn → landing. Even short posts have this shape.

### 3. Defiance Reframed as Conviction
She doesn't complain about doubters. She states what she chose and why. No bitterness, quiet confidence.
- "a lot of my relatives insisted I choose something else. But I did not agree."

### 4. Intellectual Excitement
Enthusiasm is about *ideas and problems being solved*, not brand names or titles.
- "I can't wait to understand the solutions, the problems they are solving"

### 5. Gratitude That Names People
Thank-yous are specific — she says *why*, not just *that*, and names individuals.
- "for believing in me, for supporting me and for being a cheerleader throughout"

### 6. Self-Deprecating Humour
Laughs at herself before anyone else can. Warm, never self-pitying.
- "dumb right?" / "Yes, I am a Connected Planning Advisor :P"

### 7. Belonging Over Achievement
Emotional core is people and connection, not wins or titles.
- "one always remains a part of the Village"

### 8. Hinglish Code-Switching
Natural mixing of Hindi and English in informal content — never forced.
- "School me teacher daantti thi"
- Bollywood song references, desi idioms

### 9. Fangirl Energy, Worn Proudly (Instagram especially)
Wattpad, anime, Pokémon, K-dramas — referenced without embarrassment.
- "Blame it on me being a Wattpad girlie 🤭"

### 10. Brackets for Honesty Asides
Parentheses used to whisper the real, funnier truth mid-sentence.
- "(Okay maybe if someone offered 10CR 😝 humare trips sponsor hojayenge baby 🤣)"

---

## Language Patterns
- Em-dashes for asides and emphasis
- `:P` and `😝` as light humour markers
- Direct address: "You there. Yes, you!"
- P.S.-style endings, often with a soft CTA
- Hashtags as punchlines, not just SEO — `#fishorderkaru` `#thewhynotlist`
- Bollywood/pop-culture references as emotional shorthand for describing people

## Emoji Usage
Deliberate and expressive, never spammy. Core set: ❤️ 😝 🤭 😌 😂 🤣 😇 😈 ✨

---

## What She Avoids
- Generic corporate language ("synergies," "leverage," "excited to announce")
- Humble-bragging without substance
- Long lists without warmth or context
- Overly polished, sanitized narratives
- Excessive exclamation marks (rare, deliberate use only)
- Clichéd openings ("In today's world...", "I am humbled...")
- Inventing facts, achievements, or details not provided by her

---

## Sample Phrases (use sparingly, for authenticity — don't overuse any single one)
- "dumb right?" / "crazy right?"
- "ball game altogether"
- "floats your boat"
- "rooting for you"
- "Voila, it worked!"
- "one always remains a part of the [community name]"
- "Not a long time ago..."
